Built Around How You Live

One button for "movie night". One for "everyone's home".

A home CBUS system means the switches actually match how your household uses each room — a single wall plate button drops the living area into "movie night" (dim the downlights, kill the hallway light, leave the kitchen glow on), or takes the whole house to "away" as you head out the door.

It's not a gadget bolted on top of your existing switches — it replaces them with a proper wired control network, so it looks and feels like a normal part of the house, not a smart-home add-on.

FAQ

Residential CBUS questions, answered.

Can CBUS be added during a home renovation?

Yes — a renovation is the most cost-effective time to add or extend CBUS, since the control cable can go in before walls and ceilings close up. If you've got a reno coming up, get us involved early.

Will it work with our existing switches and dimmers?

CBUS wall plates replace standard switches at each point, so existing dimmers and switches are swapped out as part of the install — we don't try to bridge old and new switching in the same circuit.

What does a typical home system cost?

Highly dependent on how many rooms and points you want controlled. A single living area with a few scenes is a modest job; a whole-house system with wall plates in every room is a bigger scope. We quote on site after walking the property — no charge for the visit.

Does CBUS add value when selling the home?

It's a genuine electrical asset rather than a gadget, so yes — a well-documented, properly wired system is a selling point, not something a buyer has to figure out or rip out.

Can we still control it from our phones as well as the wall?

Yes — app and voice control can sit alongside the wall plates. Most households end up using the wall plates day-to-day and the app for away-from-home control.
