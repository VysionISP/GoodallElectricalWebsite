Common Jobs

The work we do every week.

The mix shifts week to week — what doesn't shift is doing the job once and doing it properly.

Tripping switchboard or RCD

Modern appliances and EV chargers ask more of older boards than they were designed for. We test, identify the fault circuit, and either rebalance or upgrade.

Old ceramic-fuse switchboard

Pre-1990s boards usually have no proper safety switches. We swap to modern RCBOs that protect every circuit individually — quoted on site, certificate at the end.

Adding power points to a room

Renovation, new media room, garage workshop — we run the cable cleanly, install the points and balance the existing circuits so nothing trips.

Installing an EV charger

Wall-mounted EV charger installs, including supply upgrades if your existing board can't carry the load. Tesla, ChargePoint, generic 7kW or 22kW.

Rural shed / pump / three-phase

Dairy, irrigation, machinery sheds — proper IP-rated enclosures, sized cable runs, sub-boards where they make sense.

Compliance + safety inspections

Pre-purchase inspections, RCD testing, exit/emergency lighting, test & tag for commercial sites.

FAQ

Newry questions, answered straight.

Do you offer same-day electrical service in Newry?

Usually yes, depending on the day and what's already booked in — Newry is well within our normal run from Sale. Call 03 4130 5012 and we'll give you a straight answer on timing.

How much does it cost to call an electrician out?

During business hours (Mon–Fri 7am–5pm, excluding public holidays) the service call is $149 inc GST and includes the first 30 minutes of labour on site. After that we bill at $125 per hour + GST in 30-minute blocks. Outside business hours it's $195 per hour + GST, charged from when we leave our Sale warehouse to when we return.

Is it time to replace my switchboard?

If your board still has ceramic fuses, no safety switches on lighting circuits, or trips when appliances run together, it's probably overdue. We diagnose on site, give you a clear quote for a like-for-like replacement, and handle the DNSP notification and compliance paperwork at the end.

Can you install an EV charger at my home?

Yes. We do the cable run, mount the charger, configure it, and if your existing board can't carry the extra load we'll quote a supply upgrade at the same time. Most domestic installs come in under a day.

Do you do commercial electrical work too?

Yes — pubs, clubs, retail fitouts, motels, small office spaces and trades workshops. Test & tag, RCD testing, emergency lighting maintenance, and ongoing maintenance contracts are all part of what we do.
