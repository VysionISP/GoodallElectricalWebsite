What Is CBUS?

A wired network, not a wireless workaround.

CBUS is a low-voltage control network that runs alongside your home's wiring, linking switches, dimmers, sensors and relays on a dedicated data cable instead of relying on Wi-Fi or a cloud hub. Every switch on the network can control every light, in any combination — so one button can trigger a "movie night" scene across an entire living area, and a schedule can dim outdoor lighting at dusk without anyone touching a switch.

Because it's wired and Australian-designed for local conditions, it's the system a lot of electricians reach for on new builds and full renovations, where the cabling can go in before the walls close up.

What CBUS Can Do

One network, the whole house.

Once the network's in, adding a new function is usually a programming change, not a re-wire.

Lighting scenes & dimming

Preset scenes for dinner, movies, entertaining or bedtime — triggered from a wall plate, app, or schedule.

Schedules & timers

Outdoor lights on at dusk, hallway lights off at midnight, holiday-mode simulation while you're away.

Climate integration

Trigger heating, cooling or fans from the same panel and schedule as your lighting.

Motorised blinds & curtains

Open, close or set to a percentage on schedule or with one button on the way out the door.

App & voice control

Control from your phone, a wall-mounted touch panel, or voice assistants where you want them.

Security & access triggers

Lights that respond to a door sensor or alarm event — a simple, effective deterrent while you're out.

Wall Plate Designs

Switches that suit the room, not just the wiring.

CBUS wall plates come in a range of finishes and button layouts, so the control point looks intentional rather than bolted on.

Finishes

White, silver, matte black, and architectural finishes to match fittings and joinery.

Button Layouts

1 to 8-button plates, dimmer plates, and blank cover plates for future-proofed points you're not using yet.

Why Wired Over Wireless

Built to outlast the app.

Consumer smart-home kits are quick to install but depend on Wi-Fi, a cloud service, and an app that might not exist in five years. A wired CBUS network keeps working locally regardless of your internet connection, doesn't need batteries changed in every switch, and adds real value to the property because it's a proper electrical installation, not a gadget the next owner has to re-pair.

  • Works without an internet connection
  • No batteries, no dropped Wi-Fi devices
  • Reprogram functions without rewiring
  • A proper electrical asset, not a gadget
FAQ

CBUS questions, answered.

How much does a CBUS system cost?

Depends heavily on how many points you're controlling and whether it's new-build or retrofit. A single-room lighting scene setup is a small job; a whole-house system with wall plates, climate and blind control across every room is a much bigger scope. We quote on site after walking the property. Service-call rate during business hours is $149 inc GST for the first 30 minutes, then $125 per hour + GST; after-hours is $195 per hour + GST.

Can CBUS be retrofitted into an existing home?

Yes, though it's more involved than a new build since the control cable needs to be run to each switch location. It's most cost-effective during a renovation when walls or ceilings are already open — we'll tell you honestly if a retrofit makes sense for your job or if a different approach suits better.

Does CBUS work with other automation gear we already have?

Usually yes — CBUS integrates with most modern AV, climate and access-control equipment through standard interfaces. We'll assess what you've got and bridge it in rather than replacing gear that's already working.

Can we change what the switches do later?

Yes — that's the main advantage over hard-wired switching. Reassigning a button to a new scene or function is a programming change, not new cabling, so the system can adapt as the way you use each room changes.

Who maintains and reprograms it afterwards?

We do — as the installer we keep a record of your configuration and can make changes or add new points down the track. We also offer maintenance contracts if you want a standing arrangement.
