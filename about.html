About Us

Family-owned.
Gippsland-based.

Goodall Electrical was started in Sale with a simple idea: do the job properly, quote it honestly, and turn up when we say we will.

Our Story

Started local. Stayed local.

What began as residential electrical work around Sale has grown into a full commercial trade outfit — but the way we operate hasn't changed. We're still the same crew, still answering our own phones, still turning up to quote the job ourselves.

Today we handle everything from switchboard upgrades in family homes to full AV and automation fitouts for pubs, clubs and racing venues across Gippsland — without ever losing the local, straight-talking way we started.
