The venue AV checklist before you renovate.

3 July 2026 · 6 min read · Goodall Electrical Team

We've fitted out enough pubs, clubs and function rooms across Gippsland to see the same mistake repeated: AV gets treated as a finishing touch instead of part of the build. By the time someone asks where the screens are going, the power and data have usually already gone in the wrong wall.

Plan power before you plan screens

A wall of racing screens or a commercial sound rig draws more than a couple of double power points can handle safely. Map out your AV load — screens, amps, POS terminals, gaming machines — before the electrical rough-in, not after.

Cable for where screens will actually go

Screen placement almost always shifts once furniture and sightlines are finalised. We run data and power to more points than you think you'll need during the rough-in stage — it's far cheaper than opening a finished wall to add one more feed six months later.

Separate your networks

POS, EFTPOS, CCTV, gaming and guest Wi-Fi should never share one flat network. A renovation is the right time to put in a properly segmented network with its own comms room — not a switch sitting on top of a fridge.

Sound needs zoning, not just volume

Bistro and bar areas need different sound levels and often different content entirely. Zoned audio with independent control per area avoids the common complaint of "too loud near the tables, can't hear it at the bar."

  • Map AV power load before the electrical rough-in
  • Run more cable points than the current floor plan needs
  • Build a dedicated, segmented network — not a shared flat one
  • Zone audio by area, not by the whole venue
  • Get the AV and electrical trades talking to each other early
