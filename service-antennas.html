The Gear We Install

Built for Gippsland conditions.

We fit Matchmaster antennas as standard — an Australian brand that handles fringe-area signal, salt air and Gippsland storms far better than the cheap import gear you'll find at a hardware store. They're more expensive, they last longer, and we don't have to keep coming back to them.

If a previous install used something cheaper and it's failed early, we'll usually recommend Matchmaster as the replacement. The cost difference pays for itself well inside the warranty period.

Common Problems

Reception trouble we fix every week.

Half of antenna call-outs aren't a bad antenna — they're something smaller and cheaper. We diagnose first, then quote.

No signal or pixelated picture

Bad signal is usually a misaligned antenna, a dead amplifier, or a splitter that's quietly failed. We diagnose with a signal meter, not guesswork.

Reception drops in storms or wind

Loose mounts, water in connectors, or a tired antenna in a fringe area. We replace the weak point — not the whole system.

Some TVs work, others don't

Usually a distribution problem — a bad splitter, weak amplifier, or a cable run that's been chewed up. We trace it room by room.

Adding a TV to a new room

Need a point in the bedroom, garage or extension? We pull cable, terminate the wall plate and balance the signal so the existing TVs still run clean.

Storm-damaged or fallen antennas

Antenna down after a front came through? We replace it cleanly — new mast, new bracketing, sealed connectors so the next storm doesn't repeat the job.

FAQ

Antenna questions, answered straight.

Do you offer same-day antenna service?

For most of central Gippsland — Sale, Maffra, Stratford, Heyfield, Rosedale — yes, when we have a van free. Call 03 4130 5012 and we'll give you a straight answer.

How much does a new TV antenna installation cost?

During business hours (Monday to Friday, 7am to 5pm, excluding public holidays) a service call is $149 inc GST and includes the first 30 minutes of labour on site. After that we bill at $125 per hour + GST in 30-minute blocks. Outside business hours — weekends, evenings and public holidays — it's $195 per hour + GST, charged from when we leave our Sale warehouse to when we return. Materials (Matchmaster antenna, mast, brackets and cable) are quoted on site so there are no surprises.

Can you fix my reception without replacing the antenna?

Often yes. Many reception problems are a failed amplifier, water in a connector, a bad splitter or a misaligned antenna — all fixable for far less than a full replacement. We diagnose with a signal meter before recommending parts.

Can you add a TV point to another room?

Yes. We pull the cable, terminate the wall plate, and rebalance the existing distribution so the new point doesn't pull signal from the rest of the house.

What areas do you cover?
