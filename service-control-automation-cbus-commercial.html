Built for Venues, Not Just Rooms

One button for open, one for close.

Staff shouldn't need training to run the lighting. A commercial CBUS system means "open", "close", "event mode" and "cleaning" are each one press on a wall plate — no dimmer guesswork, no lights left blazing after the last customer leaves.

It integrates with the AV, POS and building management systems you're already running, so lighting becomes one more thing the venue does automatically instead of one more thing staff have to remember.

Why Venues Choose CBUS

Runs the venue, not the roster.

FAQ

Commercial CBUS questions, answered.

Can CBUS integrate with our existing BMS or AV system?

Usually yes — CBUS integrates with most modern building management, AV and access-control equipment through standard interfaces. We'll assess what you've got and bridge it in rather than replacing gear that's already working.

How much does a venue-wide system cost?

Depends on the venue size and how many zones you're controlling. A single-zone lighting controller is a one-day install; a full venue system with custom panels across multiple areas is weeks of design, install and commissioning. We quote on site.

Will there be downtime during installation?

We schedule work around your trading hours wherever possible and stage the install zone by zone so the venue can keep operating. We'll be upfront about any unavoidable downtime before you sign off.

Does it actually help reduce our energy bill?

Done properly, yes — typically 15-30% off the lighting and HVAC bill in hospitality venues, mostly from scheduling and auto-shutdown rather than any clever gear. Payback is usually 12-24 months.

Who do our staff call if a scene needs changing?

Us — as the installer we keep a record of your configuration and can reprogram scenes or add new points without touching the wiring. We also offer maintenance contracts if you want a standing arrangement.
