FAQ

Automation questions, answered.

Do I need expensive custom-coded gear, or are there standard products?

Both work. For most venues a packaged system (Crestron Home, KNX, or a similar standard) costs less and is easier to service. We only go custom when the brief genuinely needs it — bespoke is fun to install but expensive to maintain.

Can you integrate with our existing AV / lighting?

Usually yes. Most modern AV and lighting gear has open control protocols (Crestron, RS-232, KNX, DMX, DALI, IP). We bridge what works into the new system rather than replacing equipment unnecessarily.

How much does an automation system cost?

Heavily dependent on the venue size and what you're integrating. A simple multi-zone lighting controller is a one-day install; a full venue control system with custom panels is weeks of design + install + commissioning. We quote on site. Service-call rate during business hours is $149 inc GST for the first 30 minutes, then $125 per hour + GST; after-hours is $195 per hour + GST.

Will it actually save energy?

Done properly, yes — typically 15-30% off the lighting + HVAC bill in hospitality venues, mostly from scheduling and auto-shutdown rather than from any clever gear. The payback is usually 12-24 months.

Who maintains it after the install?

We do, if you want — we offer ongoing service contracts that cover software updates, panel re-tuning when seasons change, and remote diagnostics when something acts up. You can also self-manage if you've got the appetite.
